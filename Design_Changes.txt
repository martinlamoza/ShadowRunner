\outl0\strokewidth0 \strokec2 Design Changes\
The final version of the game changed quite a bit from the original one-pager. The original idea, Shadow Escape, was planned as a top-down survival game where the player moved around a dark arena while shadow enemies continuously chased them. The goal was to survive as long as possible while more enemies spawned and became faster over time.\
During development, I changed the game into a side-scrolling runner called ShadowRunner. Instead of moving freely in an arena, the player now moves through a level while being chased from behind by a larger shadow ghost. Smaller ghost enemies also spawn in front of the player, so the player has to avoid or jump over them while continuing to move forward.\
I also added a lives system. The player starts with four lives. Colliding with one of the smaller ghosts removes one life, while being caught by the large shadow ghost results in an immediate game over. Heart pickups can restore lives during the level. This creates two different types of danger: the smaller enemies gradually wear the player down, while the large ghost acts as a constant threat that cannot be allowed to catch up.\
The game now has a defined ending instead of continuing forever, with a finish line that allows the player to win if they reach it before losing all of their lives or being caught by the large shadow ghost.\
The visual direction stayed similar to the original idea. I kept the darker, spooky atmosphere and used shadow/ghost enemies, layered backgrounds, and parallax movement to give the environment more depth. I also added sound effects for enemy interactions, taking damage, collecting hearts, and winning. A very quiet background track is used mainly for atmosphere and is kept low enough that the gameplay sound effects can still be clearly heard.\
The main reason for these changes was that the side-scrolling format gave the game a clearer sense of progression and created more variety in the gameplay while still keeping the original idea of escaping from shadow enemies.}
